Privacy policy

In short: this site does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no form, no cookie, no audience measurement tool and no targeted advertising.

The publisher does not receive, process or keep any data about you. Your progress, avatar and settings stay on your device (see below) and are never transmitted.

Like any web server, the host (GitHub) may log technical information when you visit (IP address, date, requested page) and process it outside the European Union. This processing is GitHub's own: see its privacy statement.

External content

Fonts, images and 3D models are served by the site itself: no third-party service is contacted automatically. Links to other sites (for example roadmap.sh) are only followed if you click them; those sites have their own policies.

Your rights

Since the publisher holds no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on their side. For any question, use the contact above. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority).
