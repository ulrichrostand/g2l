Privacy policy

In short: this site does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no form, no cookie and no targeted advertising. Only an anonymous audience measurement is active (see below), which you can refuse.

In short: an account is optional. If you create one, the site keeps your email address, a fingerprint of your password (never the password itself), your course enrollments and your progress. There is no targeted advertising and no tracking cookie. An anonymous audience measurement is active (see below), which you can refuse.

What the publisher does

The publisher does not receive, process or keep any data about you. Your progress, avatar and settings stay on your device (see below) and are never transmitted.

Your account (optional)

  • Data controller: the site publisher (contact above).
  • Data: email address; password fingerprint (Argon2, cannot be reversed); account creation and last sign-in dates; courses you are enrolled in; game progress (save data); session token.
  • Purposes: creating and managing your account, saving your progress and giving it back on your devices, counting enrollments per course (a total, never a list of people).
  • Legal basis: the service you ask for (the account is needed to provide it). It stays optional: the game works without an account, with your browser's save data.
  • Retention: as long as your account exists. Deletion is immediate on request (see “Your rights”); technical database backups are purged within 14 days.
  • Recipients: no third party. Data is stored with the host named above, which does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Security: encrypted connection (HTTPS), hashed password, secure cookie session.

None of this data is sold or used for advertising.

Audience measurement

To learn how many people use the site and which pages interest them, an anonymous, cookie-free audience measurement is active. It uses the GoatCounter service, with no third-party script: the site simply sends an image request to g2l.goatcounter.com.

What is measured

  • The page visited, its title, and the domain name of the site you came from (not the full address).
  • A few actions: a click on “Play”, the start of a campaign, a completed sector (sector identifier). No text you type is sent.
  • Your screen size (three numbers), to tell whether the site is used on a phone or a computer.
  • What the measurement server infers from the request itself: browser and device type, approximate country. According to its publisher, GoatCounter does not keep IP addresses and sets no cookie or persistent identifier (its policy).

What is not

Your progress, avatar, settings and the contents of your local storage are never sent. The data is not combined with other processing nor used for targeted advertising. Nothing is measured if your browser sends “Do Not Track”.

Your choice

You can refuse this measurement at any time; your choice is remembered on this device.

Storage on your device

The site uses your browser's storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) only to provide the service you ask for. This information stays on your device, is not a tracking cookie and is never sent to the site.
